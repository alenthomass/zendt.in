Our mission

Give every Indian freelancer one quiet workspace for invoicing, clients, and income — so they spend less time on admin and more time on the work that pays.

01

Software, not a bank

Zendt is a toolkit. We help you invoice and track income — we don't hold or move your money. Payments on invoices are processed by Zwitch, an RBI-authorized payment aggregator.

02

One workspace

Invoices, clients, milestone billing, and income overview in one place — instead of a spreadsheet, a generic invoice tool, and a separate client list.

03

Built to last

Dependable, focused tools with no bloat. We build for freelancers who need clarity on what's billed, what's paid, and what's still outstanding.
