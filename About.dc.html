About Zendt

Built and registered in India. Focused tools for freelancers who work with clients around the world.

Who we are

Zendt is invoicing and income-tracking software for Indian freelancers and independent professionals who bill clients across India and abroad. We are operated by Zendt Payments Private Limited, a company incorporated in India in April 2025.

We are not a bank. Zendt does not hold, move, or have custody of your money. When your clients pay an invoice, payment processing is handled by Zwitch, a licensed payment aggregator regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

What we build

A single workspace for the work that usually gets scattered: creating and sending invoices, tracking clients, billing in milestones, and seeing what's been paid and what's still outstanding. Nothing more complicated than that — just dependable software that stays out of your way.
