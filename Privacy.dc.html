Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Zendt is a registered Indian software company. This policy explains what information we collect through our invoicing and income-tracking software, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect account information you provide (such as your name, email, and business details), invoice and client data you enter into the software, and basic usage data (like device and browser information) that helps us maintain and improve the Service.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to operate the Service — creating and displaying your invoices, generating your income dashboard, keeping your account secure, responding to support requests, and communicating important product updates.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your data. We may share limited information with service providers who help us run the Service (such as hosting and email delivery), and only to the extent needed for them to perform that work. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Storage & Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information stored in the Service. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

6. Your Choices

You can access, update, or delete most of your account and invoice information directly within the software. You may also contact us to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to understand how the Service is used, so we can improve it over time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post any changes here with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@zendt.in or via our Contact page.
