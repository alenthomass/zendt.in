Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Zendt Payments Private Limited ("Zendt," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, join our waitlist, contact us, or use our invoicing and income-tracking software (the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Account and profile information — such as your name, email, and business details when you register or use the Service.
  • Waitlist information — your email address when you join our early-access waitlist on the website.
  • Contact form information — your name, email, topic, and message when you submit our contact form.
  • Invoice and client data — information you enter into the Service, including client details, invoice content, and income records.
  • Usage and device data — basic technical information such as browser type, device identifiers, and pages visited, to operate and improve the Service.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to operate the Service, respond to enquiries, manage waitlist communications, process support requests, maintain security, and send important product updates. We do not sell your personal data.

4. How We Share Information

We may share limited information with service providers who help us run the Service, only to the extent needed for them to perform that work. This includes:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that store and deliver the website and software.
  • Email and form delivery providers used to deliver contact-form submissions and operational emails.
  • Zwitch, an RBI-authorized payment aggregator, for processing client invoice payments initiated through the Service. Zendt does not hold client funds; payment data needed to process invoices is handled by Zwitch under its own terms and policies.
  • Other providers involved in subscription billing, analytics, or customer support, where applicable.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Zendt, our users, or others.

5. Data Storage & Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information stored in the Service. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

6. Your Choices

You can access, update, or delete most of your account and invoice information directly within the software. You may also contact us to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to understand how the Service is used, so we can improve it over time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post any changes here with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@zendt.in or via our Contact page.
