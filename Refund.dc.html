Refund Policy

1. Scope

This policy covers refunds for paid subscriptions to the Zendt software. It does not apply to funds, invoices, or transactions between you and your own clients — Zendt is invoicing and tracking software, and has no role in your client relationships beyond providing the tool.

2. Subscription Refunds

If you're unsatisfied with a paid Zendt subscription, contact us within 7 days of the charge and we'll review your request. Eligible requests are typically refunded to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

3. Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancelling stops future billing at the end of your current billing period; we don't provide prorated refunds for partial periods unless required by law.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

We generally don't issue refunds for subscription periods already substantially used, or for dissatisfaction unrelated to the functioning of the software itself.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email hello@zendt.in with your account details and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
